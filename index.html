    How to play

    1. The block falls on its own. Every level has a queue of pieces; each falls from the top at a steady rate. Fit them all below the dashed line and the level is cleared — the arena is meant to end packed.
    2. Swipe left or right across the arena to shift the piece one column. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys.
    3. Tap the arena to rotate. On a keyboard, press space.
    4. Swipe down (or hold the down arrow) to speed the fall while you decide.
    5. Undo unlocks the last piece if you place it wrong. The brown ground at the bottom is the arena's landscape — each column has its own floor height, and pieces stack on top of it just as they do on each other.

    Stars are for a clean run. Finish without undoing for three; a few undos still earn two; finishing at all earns one.
